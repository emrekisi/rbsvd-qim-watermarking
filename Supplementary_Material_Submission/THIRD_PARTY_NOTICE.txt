The package includes the image files needed to reproduce the reported
experiments.  The eight classic host images were obtained from G. Levkine's
Test Images Collection and the Standard Test Images for Image Processing
repository:
https://www.hlevkin.com/hlevkin/06testimages.htm
https://github.com/mohammadimtiazz/standard-test-images-for-Image-Processing

The Kodak24 files are from the Kodak Lossless True Color Image Suite.  The
four supplied watermark pixel arrays were prepared by the authors for this
study.  This notice records provenance and does not characterize the host or
Kodak images as public domain, copyright-free, or license-free.
